SalonX

StoreHub · Sole product manager · 2026 to now

An all-in-one platform that beauty salons in Japan run their day on. Four connected apps: a web admin for owners, a point of sale at the counter, a staff app, and customer booking on the web and LINE.

What I did

  • Ran the whole product function alone: product, design, release rhythm and analytics across all 4 apps.
  • Wrote 55 requirement documents and 36 specs, and designed the flows myself from prototype to production.
  • Led the Hot Pepper Beauty integration, from a stack of vendor API documents to a live, synced salon.
  • Built an AI-assisted way of working (30 agent skills, 7 one-command pipelines) for triage, research, spec drafting and prototyping.
  • Merged my own fixes into the production app when small gaps would otherwise have waited behind feature work.

Impact delivered

  • 23 releases in 6 months, one every 7.6 days.
  • Monthly bookings grew from 42 to more than 3,400 in the same 6 months.
  • 112 new features and 121 improvements shipped by the team to that rhythm.
  • The first salon is live with Hot Pepper Beauty sync, which lets a salon adopt SalonX without leaving Japan's main booking site.
  • Specs now get written 3 times faster, and the product soft-launched in Thailand in September 2026.

SalonX sales insight portal

Internal tool · Designed and built solo · 2026

Turns recorded sales calls into evidence for product decisions: which salons SalonX suits, why deals are won or lost, and which features people keep asking for. Every number links back to the calls behind it.

What I did

  • Analysed 83 recorded sales calls with 72 prospects, straight from the Japanese transcripts.
  • Built a 6-step AI pipeline: transcription, structured reading of each call, two independent blind checks, and a final referee.
  • Linked the calls to the sales system and checked every requested feature against what is actually built.
  • Built a phone page in Japanese where the sales rep confirms or corrects the AI's reading.
  • Rewrote every screen in plain language so non-specialists can use it.

Impact delivered

  • 196 distinct feature requests catalogued, with the unmet ones ranked and fed into the roadmap.
  • Sales can now tell which lost deals were lost on product and which were winnable.
  • 5 screens replaced a diagnosis document too long for anyone to re-read.
  • Reviewed and taken up by the product, country and sales leads.

E-invoicing for Malaysian merchants

StoreHub · Product manager · 2024 to 2026

Malaysia made e-invoicing mandatory in phases from August 2024. StoreHub's e-invoice stack lets merchants comply from BackOffice: a customer scans the QR code on their receipt to request an e-invoice, and every unclaimed sale goes into one automatic monthly submission to the tax authority.

What I did

  • Owned the design and delivery of the stack, from merchant setup and issuing to internal compliance operations.
  • Wrote the requirements for the e-invoice API, company profiles and submission, payout fee e-invoices and self-billed e-invoices.
  • Extended coverage from in-store sales to online orders.
  • Settled open tax treatment questions directly with the tax authority's helpdesk.
  • Led a correctness review in 2026 and corrected the help centre guidance.

Impact delivered

  • The final phase went live for merchants ahead of the 1 July 2025 mandate.
  • Merchants switch e-invoicing on themselves in 5 steps, with no call to support.
  • Unclaimed sales are consolidated and submitted every month without the merchant lifting a finger.
  • StoreHub's own payout fee invoices are submitted automatically too.
